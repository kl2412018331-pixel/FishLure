CLIENT QUICK GUIDE

How to use FishLure

Use the advisor for a lure idea, then save recommendations or keep a record of your catches. You do not need fishing experience to get started.

Get a lure recommendation

  1. Go to the lure advisor.
  2. Search for the fish you hope to catch, then choose it from the list.
  3. Choose the closest answers for water clarity, weather, and where you will fish. A best guess is fine.
  4. Select Show me a lure to try. Read the top suggestion for a lure photo, color, depth, and how to move the lure.
  5. Look through the other lure ideas or adjust your answers to get a different suggestion.
Keep in mind: recommendations are a starting point, not a guarantee of a catch. Fish and conditions can change what works.

Create an account or sign in

Select Log in / Register in the website header. Create an account with your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters, or sign in with your existing account. The lure advisor works without an account; signing in is needed for the personal features below.

Use your fishing notebook

Save a recommendation

After getting a suggestion, select Save this recommendation. Your top suggestion is saved to your account and appears under Saved recommendations. Select Delete on an entry to remove it.

Record a catch

In Catch log, select the fish species and enter the date and location. Size, lure, depth, and notes are optional. Select Add catch to my log. You can delete an entry from the log later.

Send feedback

Choose a topic, enter a short subject and details, then select Send to FishLure. You need to be signed in to send feedback.

Need help?

  • If a personal feature asks you to sign in, log in and try again.
  • If the fish list or recommendation cannot load, contact the FishLure site administrator.
  • Sign out on a shared computer, and do not share your password.
